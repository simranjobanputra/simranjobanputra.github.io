About

I build thoughtful products and bring creative ideas to life.

I’m a product-focused designer with a business background who loves getting to the heart of a problem, shaping the vision, and bringing ideas to life. I enjoy working at the intersection of product strategy, design, and creativity — from figuring out what to build to getting my hands dirty making it happen.

Most recently, I was the Founding Designer at Ray, where I helped take a product from the ground up to an App Store launch, growing it to 900 users. I love the challenge of building something from scratch, wearing different hats, and figuring things out along the way. I consider myself a lifelong learner.

Product Strategy & Design

I believe great design starts well before opening a design tool. I’m drawn to understanding the business, digging into user problems, and connecting the dots between what people need and what makes a product successful. I love turning ambiguous ideas into clear product direction and bringing that vision to life through thoughtful, hands-on design.
