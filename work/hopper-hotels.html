Hopper

Redesigning the hotel shopping experience

Hopper’s hotel list was cluttered with banners and pop-ups that buried the actual listings. I simplified and standardized the list so shoppers could see hotels first and trust what they were seeing.

Results

14%increase in conversion (goal: 9%)
20%increase in purchase frequency
100%of users see 2+ listings on first scroll

The challenge

Banners and pop-ups were hiding the hotels

Nearly 35% of shoppers never saw a single listing, which led to a 65% drop-off in bookings. In interviews, users described the app as cluttered and hard to trust.

The solution

One system, with hotels first

I cleared out the top of the list, created rules for banners, and moved the map to a floating button, so the first thing shoppers see when they scroll is hotels.

Process

From 20+ interviews to a banner system

Here’s how I went from research to the banner system we shipped.

1 / 3

What research told us

I started with past data and a competitive analysis to form my hypotheses, then validated them in 20+ interviews.

5%use the map on the list
80%of those map users book
62%leave over banners and pop-ups
Photosdrive decisions

2 / 3

Balancing user and business needs

Users wanted the banners gone, but 40% of app revenue came from what they advertised. I explored four ways to keep banners without getting in the way of shopping.

3 / 3

A banner system

I created a banner style guide with fixed sizes, one image and one call to action, and placed banners between listings. This led to higher conversion and higher intent from shoppers who tapped on them.
