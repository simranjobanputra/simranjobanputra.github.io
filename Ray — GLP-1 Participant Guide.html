Getting started

Setting up Ray

Before your first workout, get Ray fully set up so it can personalize everything to you.

1
Download the Ray app
Available on iOS. Search for "Ray AI Trainer" and install.
2
Complete onboarding
Ray will ask about your fitness goals, experience level, and what kind of training you enjoy. Take your time — your answers shape everything. When asked about medications or health context, please note that you are on a GLP-1 — this helps Ray tailor your program to how your body is responding.
Important
3
Set your gym environment & equipment
Tell Ray where you work out — commercial gym, home gym, outdoors — and what equipment you have access to. Your workouts will be built around exactly what's available to you.
4
Pick your 2 workout days
Choose the two days each week that work best for you. You can always adjust these inside Ray if your schedule changes.
5
Join the WhatsApp support group
Use the link above to join. This is where you can ask questions, flag anything that's not working, and connect with others in the program.
Required
6
Complete your first pre-workout check-in
Before each of your 2 weekly sessions, fill out the quick pre-workout check-in above. It takes under a minute and helps Ray understand how you're feeling that day.
Week by week

What you'll learn

Each week builds on the last. Here's what to focus on as you go.

Week 1 Getting Set Up Foundation
Your focus this week
  • Complete onboarding and do your first two Ray workouts
  • Get familiar with how Ray structures a session — warm-up, main work, cool-down
  • Fill out the pre-workout survey before each session and the weekly check-in at the end of the week
  • Join the WhatsApp group and introduce yourself
  • Notice how your body feels during and after workouts on GLP-1 — energy, appetite, recovery
This week's goal: Just show up. The workouts don't need to be perfect — getting through them and filling out your check-ins is the win.
Week 2 Make Ray Your Personal Trainer Personalize
Your focus this week
  • Start talking to Ray mid-session — ask it to swap an exercise, scale a movement up or down, or explain why it chose something
  • Tell Ray what feels good and what doesn't — it remembers, adapts, and gets more specific to you over time
  • Explore training styles beyond your default plan and find what actually resonates with how your body is feeling on GLP-1
  • Adjust your schedule inside Ray if your week changes — your trainer fits around your life, not the other way around
This week's goal: Ray isn't a generic workout app — it's your personal trainer. The more you communicate with it, the more it becomes specific to you. Start that relationship this week.
Week 3 Thinking About Progress Learn
Your focus this week
  • Learn what progressive overload means — gradually increasing the demand on your muscles over time
  • Start tracking how weights, reps, and sets are shifting week-to-week (Ray does this for you — start noticing it)
  • Understand why strength training matters especially on GLP-1 — preserving and building muscle while losing weight
  • Notice the difference between a workout that was hard because of effort vs. hard because your body wasn't ready
This week's goal: Shift your mindset from "completing workouts" to "getting stronger." Progress on GLP-1 looks different — and that's a good thing.
Week 4 Measuring Your Progress Reflect
Your focus this week
  • Look back at Week 1 — compare how workouts feel now vs. when you started
  • Review the weights and reps from your first sessions — notice what's changed
  • Think beyond the scale: energy levels, how you move, strength, endurance, and how GLP-1 side effects have evolved
  • Complete your final weekly check-in with as much detail as you can — your feedback shapes what comes next
  • Share your biggest win from the 4 weeks in the WhatsApp group
This week's goal: Reflect honestly. Progress isn't always linear — but 4 weeks of consistent movement, especially on GLP-1, is genuinely significant. Acknowledge it.
